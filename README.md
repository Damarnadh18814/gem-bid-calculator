# GeM Bid Calculator

A free auction-theory calculator for GeM, CPPP, state e-procurement and any other lowest-price (L1) tender. By The Game Theory Desk.

**Use it:** BRAND-LINK

It finds the bid that maximises **expected profit = margin × chance of winning**, in two ways:

- **From past results:** paste the winning L1 prices of similar past tenders (as % of estimated value). The win chance at each price is estimated from how often past L1 prices were higher.
- **Quick estimate:** no data needed. It uses the first-price reverse-auction equilibrium, **Bid = Cost + (H − Cost) ÷ n**.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

*This is analysis, not a guarantee of winning. Check each tender's terms for MSE and Make in India preferences. Never coordinate prices with rivals: bid-rigging is illegal under the Competition Act, 2002.*
